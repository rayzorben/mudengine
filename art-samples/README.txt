Sample art from mudengine, 2026-10-22.

rooms/       Top-down room pictures drawn from the room's name and description
             (GreaterMUD world data). One picture per room, 512 px.
items/       Item pictures drawn from the item's name, shown in the inventory
             and shop views.
characters/  Character portraits drawn from the character's race, class, gender,
             hair, eyes and the stat sentences the game prints (health, strength,
             agility, charm, intellect, willpower).
